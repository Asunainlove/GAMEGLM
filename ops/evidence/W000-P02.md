## Architecture notes and limits

- `GameState.commit()` validates and applies every operation to a deep working copy, then replaces state and increments revision once. Failed transactions never record their source ID.
- `restore_snapshot()` is explicitly a load lifecycle operation and rejects non-fresh runtime state. Normal gameplay remains patch-only.
- Save files contain stable JSON primitives and deltas, never `Vector2`, `Node`, `Resource`, or a complete TileMap.
- Saving performs verified tmp write, primary-to-backup rotation, then tmp promotion. On rotation/promotion failure, existing primary/backup and/or the verified tmp remain available.
- Loading returns the highest valid candidate but does not auto-repair a corrupt primary yet.
- `content_hash` remains empty until the ContentDB packet supplies the gameplay content hash.
